Important. PDFVex does not create an online user account. There is no Google Login, Firebase account or developer-operated cloud document database in the current release. You can remove the app's PDF copies locally.

1. No PDFVex account to delete

PDFVex works without account registration. You do not create a PDFVex username, password or user profile, and PDFVex does not offer cloud synchronization in this version. As a result, there is no remotely hosted PDFVex user account for us to close.

This page provides the data-removal process for the information the app stores locally on your phone and explains third-party advertising data separately.

2. Delete a single document in PDFVex

  1. Open PDFVex.
  2. Open Files / My Documents and find the PDF you want to remove.
  3. Open the document's menu, choose Remove or Delete, and confirm.

PDFVex deletes its own managed PDF copy and removes the library entry. Your originally imported PDF or image is not removed. Copies exported to Downloads, cloud drives, messaging apps or other storage locations must be deleted separately.

3. Remove all PDFVex local data

  1. Open Android Settings on your device.
  2. Go to Apps → PDFVex → Storage (the labels may differ by phone brand).
  3. Select Clear storage / Clear data and confirm. Alternatively, uninstall the PDFVex app.

This generally removes locally stored PDFVex-managed documents, library metadata and preferences on that device. System backup/restore services may retain or restore copies independently. Clearing local app data does not delete separately exported or shared files.

4. What is deleted and what may remain

  • Removing one PDF: removes that app-managed PDF and its PDFVex library entry.
  • Clearing PDFVex storage: removes local app-managed documents and settings, subject to Android backup and restore behavior.
  • Original files: are not removed when you delete a PDFVex-managed copy.
  • Shared or exported copies: must be deleted from their new locations or recipient services.
  • Third-party advertising records: follow Google's separate retention and deletion policies.

5. Google AdMob and advertising data

PDFVex displays ads through Google AdMob. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may process information such as IP address, device/ad identifiers, interactions and diagnostic data for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Removing a PDF or uninstalling PDFVex does not necessarily delete Google's separate records.

To manage or delete eligible ad-related information, use Google My Ad Center and your Android advertising/privacy settings. See Google's Privacy Policy for more information.

6. Questions or data requests

Since this version does not maintain an online PDFVex account database, there is no server-side account deletion request form. If you need help understanding the deletion process or have a privacy concern, contact the developer.
